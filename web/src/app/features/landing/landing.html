TEAM FUND AND MEALS

Team money everyone can follow

A running balance, dues by family and a clear ledger for the finance volunteer. Families record a payment, finance confirms it, and tournament meal budgets and sign-ups sit right alongside.

  • Each family sees the team totals and only their own dues
  • Meal sign-ups and budgets per tournament
ROLES AND PRIVACY

Private by design

Every account starts with an invite from the team, and every person sees what their role needs. Allergies, travel plans and each family's dues stay with the family and the staff who need them, never in an open spreadsheet link.

  • Invite-only: the team invites each family by email
  • Roles for admin, coach, coordinator, food, finance and parent
  • Families choose whether to share their phone and email
